Why Japanese Auctions Are the World's Best Source

Japan runs one of the most regulated, transparent vehicle markets on earth. Here's what makes it — and us — different.

01

Independent Third-Party Grading

Every vehicle is graded 1–5 by independent inspectors with no ties to the seller. The grade covers bodywork, interior, mechanical condition, and accident history — all documented on a standardised auction sheet you receive before bidding.

02

Low Mileage, Well-Maintained Vehicles

Japan's high domestic taxes and world-class public transport mean most vehicles are lightly driven and meticulously maintained. Finding a 3-year-old car under 20,000 km is common — not exceptional.

03

Zero Tolerance for Fraud

Japanese auction houses operate under strict legal frameworks. Misrepresentation of mileage, condition, or accident history is prosecuted — giving international buyers a level of protection unavailable in most other markets.

04

Massive Weekly Supply

Over 150,000 vehicles go through Japanese auctions every week across 130+ auction houses. Whatever the make, model, spec, or year — if it exists in Japan, it goes through auction.

05

Fair Market Pricing

Auction prices reflect true market value — no dealer markups, no inflated showroom prices. You pay what the market says the vehicle is worth, with full visibility into how bids are placed and settled.

06

Proven Global Track Record

Millions of vehicles have been exported from Japan to every corner of the world for decades. The system is trusted, battle-tested, and built around international buyers like you.

Container shipping fits 3–5 vehicles per container depending on size and protects your vehicle from weather — a container to Germany, for example, costs approximately $2,500. Note that containers are generally not covered by insurance, and there is a small risk of minor contact marks during loading. RO-RO (Roll-on Roll-off) is priced by the vehicle's volume at approximately $80 per cubic metre, and insurance is available for around ¥5,000 JPY. However, RO-RO is not available to every country — Mediterranean and Black Sea destinations must use container shipping. Contact us and we'll recommend the best option for your route.
