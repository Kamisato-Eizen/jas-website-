About Japan Auto Supply

We're a Japan-based vehicle export broker connecting international dealers directly to the Japanese wholesale auction market.

Japan Auto Supply is a Tokyo-based used car export brokerage. We work directly with Japan's major auction houses to source vehicles on behalf of overseas dealerships and private buyers. Our role is straightforward: we give international buyers honest, direct access to the Japanese wholesale market — without the markup, the guesswork, or the runaround.

When you come to us with a request, we search active auction listings, translate and assess condition reports, advise on realistic pricing, and place bids on your behalf. Once a vehicle is secured, we handle all export documentation and coordinate shipping to your destination port. Every cost is itemised before you commit — auction price, agent fee, inland transport, freight. No hidden charges, no surprises at customs.

We work with a selective client base and prefer to build long-term relationships over one-off transactions. If you know what you want, we'll find it. If you're still figuring it out, we'll help you get there. Reach us directly on WhatsApp for vehicle requests, availability checks, or a quote.
