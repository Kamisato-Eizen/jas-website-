Understanding Auction Grades

Japan uses a standardised grading system for all auctioned vehicles.

S / 6

As New

Less than 12 months from first registration. Exterior and interior in immaculate condition.

5

Near Perfect

Very difficult to obtain. Near perfect condition with only very minor imperfections.

4.5

Very Good

High confidence grade. May have very slight body imperfections. Mileage will be higher than a Grade 5.

4

Good

May have small scratches and dents. Above average condition, often with maintenance records. Minor repairs if any.

3.5

Above Average

The most common auction grade with a wide range of condition. Needs standard maintenance and possibly minor repairs. Pre-bid inspection recommended.

RA

Repaired Accident

Minor accident damage that has been repaired well. Condition can be comparable to a Grade 4.5. Inspection recommended.

3

Average

Noticeable scratches, paint blemishes and/or large dents. Interior may show tears, burns or stains. Body work and repairs required.

R

Accident History

Accident history with damage that may or may not have been repaired. Wide range of condition. Often associated with drift and rally cars. Inspection strongly recommended.

2

Poor

The vehicle has corrosion holes.

*** / 0 / Blank

Uninspected

Not inspected by the auction house, may have been in a major accident, may not be running, or is sold as-is with no claim.
